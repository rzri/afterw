个人网站正在悄悄往回走。博客、RSS、手搓的首页、微格式——这些零件如今都齐了，我也发现自己越来越不在乎体量，而在乎*模样*。

The personal web is quietly circling back. Blogs, RSS, hand-rolled homepages, microformats — the pieces are all here again, and I find myself caring less about scale and more about *shape*.

一条动态流优化的是"有什么新的"。对我的个人站，我要的恰恰相反：一间进门就说出房子是干嘛的、谁住在这里、好房间在哪儿的大厅。新不新是你要穿过的一条走廊，不是终点。

A feed optimises for "what's new." For a personal site I want the opposite: a foyer that says what the house is, who lives here, and where the good rooms are. Newness is a hallway you walk through, not the destination.

## 我想让首页做什么 · What I want a homepage to do

- 介绍一个活人，而不是一个数据画像。 Introduce a human, not a demographic.
- 指着那些值得回来的地方。 Point to the things worth returning to.
- 让文字站在前面，而不是躲在一串时间戳后面。 Let the writing stand up front instead of behind a stream of timestamps.

所以我让这个小站以文章开头，而不是伪装成动态流的流水账。它是一小片更小、更静的网，而我想要我这一角，住起来像是个你会想开窗的地方。

That's why this blog leads with a list of writing rather than a chronological dump disguised as a feed. It's a smaller, calmer web — and I want my corner of it to feel like somewhere you'd open a window.
