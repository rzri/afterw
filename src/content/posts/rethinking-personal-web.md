The personal web is quietly circling back. Blogs, RSS, hand-rolled homepages,
microformats — the pieces are all here again, and I find myself caring less about
scale and more about *shape*.

A feed optimises for "what's new." For a personal site I want the opposite: a
foyer that says what the house is, who lives here, and where the good rooms are.
Newness is a hallway you walk through, not the destination.

## What I want a homepage to do

- Introduce a human, not a demographic.
- Point to the things worth returning to.
- Let the writing stand up front instead of behind a stream of timestamps.

That's why this blog leads with a list of writing rather than a chronological
dump disguised as a feed. It's a smaller, calmer web, and I want my corner of it
to feel like somewhere you'd open a window.
