这两年开始,我一直在往生活里"删"东西。删掉推送,删掉连续打卡,删掉那些"别人都在做所以我也该做"的事。删着删着,竟然觉得日子越来越像自己的了。

Lately I've been subtracting — the notifications, the streaks, the "everyone's doing it so I should too." And the funny thing is, the less I have, the more my days feel like mine.

删东西不难,难的是删了之后不心慌。头一个星期,手会摸向口袋里的手机。到后来才明白,那种空不是空落落的空,是腾出来给你的。

Removing is easy; the hard part is not panicking once it's gone. For about a week my hand kept reaching for the phone in my pocket. Eventually I understood that the empty it leaves isn't a hollow — it's space handed back to you.

腾出来的空,我用来做很普通的事:煮一壶水,看一场雨,把一本旧书从头再翻一遍。这些事从前觉得是浪费时间,如今成了我一天里最坐得住的片段。

And I fill that space with the most ordinary things: boil water, watch the rain, read an old book from the start. These used to feel like a waste of time. Now they're the most settled hours of my day.

慢不是慵懒,静不是冷清。慢是给自己留白,静是让自己听得见。它们在,日子就稳。

Slow isn't laziness; still isn't loneliness. Slow is leaving yourself some blank space; still is letting yourself hear. When those two are around, the days hold steady.
